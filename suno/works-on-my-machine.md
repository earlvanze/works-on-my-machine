# Works On My Machine (Suno song sheet)

Theme: no one knows your machine like your agent does, not even you. Any number of reasons can stop
your build of the app from running on your machine. Before you open a ticket, point your agent at the
repo, the issues and the PRs, because someone has probably already filed it.

## Title
```
Works On My Machine
```

## Style of Music
```
catchy upbeat synth-pop, 124 BPM, bouncy bass, handclaps, gang vocal chant hook, playful male lead, glitchy synth stabs, earworm chorus, call and response
```
Exclude: `country, acoustic, ballad, autotune`
